      Frequently asked questions

      What fusion methods do you support for HDPE joints?

      We supply pipe compatible with butt fusion, electrofusion, and socket fusion where applicable, with parameters aligned to equipment from major manufacturers.

      How do I choose PN class and SDR for buried water mains?

      Selection depends on surge pressure, soil load, and safety factors. Share your hydraulic profile and we’ll recommend PN/SDR combinations per ISO 4427 practice.

      Can you provide mill certificates and batch traceability?

      Yes—certificates of conformity, resin lot numbers, and pressure test reports are available per delivery.

      Do you ship coils and long-length sticks?

      Both formats are available subject to transport regulations and site offloading constraints.

      What lead times should we plan for custom dimensions?

      Standard sizes are typically faster; non-catalog OD/SDR runs may require tooling review—request a timeline with your RFQ.
